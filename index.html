About Us

Founded in 2014, Aura is a proud, family-owned specialty café built on a passion for exceptional coffee, elevated flavors, and bringing people together. What started as a shared family dream has grown into a neighborhood sanctuary dedicated to serving our community with handcrafted brews, artisanal pastries, and seasonal plates prepared with care. We believe a great café is about more than just what is in the cup; it is about the community inside our doors. That is why we strive to support and give back to our local neighborhood by creating opportunities, supporting local initiatives, and cultivating lasting relationships with everyone who walks through our doors. At Aura, every guest is family, and every moment we share is crafted with heart.

Our Goal

Welcome to Aura, where culinary artistry meets the comfort of a neighborhood sanctuary. Our mission is to elevate your daily ritual by crafting exquisite, ethically sourced specialty coffees alongside a meticulously curated menu of artisanal pastries and seasonal plates. We believe that exceptional food and drink should be served with warmth, intention, and an unhurried elegance—creating a vibrant space where conversation flows, community thrives, and every visit feels like a celebrated occasion.
